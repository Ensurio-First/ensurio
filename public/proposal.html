Turning a long guide into a journey the reader wants to finish

The Jewelers Block Insurance article is thorough but dense — 16 sections of prose and bullet lists. Here is how each section could be re-shaped visually, sequenced along the AIDA path, so a jeweler moves from “I should read this” to “I should call Ensurio.”

The problem & the approach

Right now every section looks the same

Heading, paragraph, bullet list — repeated 16 times. The reader can’t tell the hook from the fine print, so attention leaks and most never reach the call-to-action at the bottom.

AIDA gives the page a spine: grab Attention, build Interest, create Desire for the cover, then drive Action. Each stage gets its own visual language, so the design itself tells the reader where they are and pulls them forward.
